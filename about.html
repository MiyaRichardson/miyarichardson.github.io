01 / The Core Obsession

I do not start my design processes inside high fidelity interfaces. I start with an empty unlined journal, messy arrows, and structural breakdowns. To me, a product succeeds when the logic hidden beneath the user journeys fits completely naturally into human habits.

Background

Bridging the Gap: Masters & Coding

With academic backing from a Master's degree, my approach to research is deeply thorough, data-led, and analytical. Crucially, because I can write code, I don't build designs in an isolated bubble. Understanding how components function on the front end means I map structures that translate flawlessly from raw layout logic directly into implementation.

How I Map ➔

Stripping down overwhelming options until only the absolute necessary structure stands clear to the end user.

How I Research ➔

Combining advanced Master's methodologies with developer logic to spot systematic design gaps.
