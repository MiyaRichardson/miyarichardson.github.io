About My Design Ethos

Miya Richardson — UX Researcher & UX Designer

I focus on transforming complex and exclusionary processes into clear digital structures. My design background centers on removing systemic barriers and digital reading blocks through logical visual mapping and plain language systems.

My Master's degree experience heavily influences this approach and sets me apart in the industry. This advanced academic training developed my skills in qualitative methodologies, empirical user research, and cognitive compliance. It allows me to bridge the gap between analytical insights and practical interactive systems, elevating my work far beyond surface aesthetics. I also bring hands-on experience with front-end development, having coded this responsive portfolio website myself to ensure accurate execution of my design concepts.

Good design does not look overly polished; it works cleanly and stands out to users because it resolves real issues. I prefer testing assumptions with users directly, looking at layouts in raw formats early on, and refining microcopy continuously to support users completely.
