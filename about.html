Design approach

I build digital products from the ground up, focusing on usability over decoration. By structuring clear navigation systems and mapping journeys to real user mental models, I design interfaces that feel natural and intuitive to navigate.

My Master’s degree grounds my work in data-driven user research and usability testing. Because I understand HTML, CSS, and development constraints, my wireframes and interactive prototypes hand over seamlessly to engineering teams.
