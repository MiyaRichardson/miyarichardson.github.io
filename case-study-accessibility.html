Accessible Health Platform

Role UX / UI Designer
Platform Responsive Web Platform
Focus Inclusive Interaction

Overview

This project explored how digital cooking and nutrition experiences could become more accessible for people with visual, cognitive, and physical impairments.

The aim was to create a clearer and more flexible experience that allowed users to navigate recipes, understand instructions, and complete preparation steps independently.

Research & Accessibility

I explored the barriers users can face when interacting with complex recipe and preparation interfaces.

This included considering visual hierarchy, cognitive load, touch targets, readable instructions, navigation patterns, and alternative ways of communicating information.

User Needs

The design was informed by different accessibility needs, including users who may have difficulty reading small text, processing dense instructions, or physically interacting with small controls.

Information Architecture

I structured the experience around clear task-based navigation so users could move between ingredients, preparation steps, and supporting information without becoming lost.

Accessible Interaction

Accessibility was considered throughout the interface, including larger interaction areas, clearer hierarchy, simplified instructions, and alternative ways to understand important information.

Ingredient Checklist

A checklist system was added to the recipe steps, with large tap targets and screen reader friendly states (Checked / Not checked).

Final Screens

A selection of final high-fidelity screens showcasing the completed interface. These demonstrate the visual system, accessibility patterns, and overall user experience brought together in the final build.
